## 2. Party Obligations

### Provider (Pinnacle Digital Solutions, Inc.)

- Design, develop, and deploy a custom ERP platform (inventory, order processing, financial reporting, CRM modules) per Exhibit A.
- Assign a dedicated project manager as Client's primary point of contact.
- Meet the seven milestone deadlines listed in Section 2.2.
- If a milestone slips by more than 30 calendar days, submit a remediation plan within 10 business days upon Client's request.
- Deliver Custom Work Product and assign exclusive ownership to Client upon full payment.
- Grant Client a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to any embedded Provider Tools.
- Implement and maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards compliant with CCPA, GDPR (where applicable), and SOC 2 Type II.
- Notify Client of any data breach or security incident within 48 hours of discovery.
- Conduct annual third-party security audits and share results upon request; cooperate with Client-initiated audits on 30 days' notice (max once per year).
- Perform Services in a professional and workmanlike manner; warrant Custom Work Product conforms to Exhibit A for 12 months post-acceptance and does not infringe third-party IP.
- Indemnify Client against third-party claims from breach of warranties, IP infringement, or Provider's gross negligence/willful misconduct.
- On termination, deliver all completed and in-progress work product and return/destroy Client's Confidential Information within 30 days.
- Maintain confidentiality for 5 years post-termination.

### Client (GreenLeaf Enterprises, LLC)

- Pay Provider a total fixed fee of $2,400,000 per the installment schedule in Section 3.1.
- Pay invoices within 30 days of receipt; late amounts accrue interest at 1.5% per month (or max legal rate, whichever is lower).
- Provide reasonable access to systems, personnel, and information necessary for Provider's performance.
- May withhold up to 10% of each milestone payment as retainage, released upon final acceptance.
- On termination, pay Provider for Services satisfactorily performed through the termination date.
- Refrain from reverse-engineering, decompiling, or disassembling Provider Tools without prior written consent.
- Hold Provider's Confidential Information in strict confidence for 5 years; use it solely for performance under the Agreement.
- Indemnify Provider for third-party claims arising from Client's misuse of the Custom Work Product or violations of law.
- Maintain confidentiality for 5 years post-termination; comply with 12-month non-solicitation covenant.

### Mutual Obligations

- Attempt good-faith negotiation for 30 days before initiating arbitration.
- Provide written notices; amendments require a signed writing by both Parties.
- Neither Party may assign without the other Party's consent, except in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets.

## 3. Risks and Concerns

### Asymmetric / Unfavorable Risks for the **Client**

1. **IP transfer conditioned on full payment (Section 4.1).** Client does not own the Custom Work Product until *all* fees are paid. If Client withholds the 10% retainage or has a payment dispute, it could temporarily lack formal ownership of what it paid millions to build — a significant leverage imbalance.
2. **Liability cap (Section 8.1).** Provider's total liability is capped at fees paid/payable. With a $2.4M contract, a catastrophic data-loss event, project abandonment, or critical IP infringement could leave Client under-compensated. Carve-outs for Sections 4, 5, and 6 help, but ordinary performance failures are tightly capped.
3. **Disclaimer of implied warranties (Section 7.3).** Provider disclaims merchantability and fitness-for-purpose warranties. The only meaningful Client protection is the 12-month conformance warranty — narrow for an ERP of this scope and value.
4. **No express service-level agreement (SLA) or uptime guarantee.** Section 7 does not specify performance benchmarks, response times, or uptime commitments. The 30-day milestone-slippage threshold is the only objective performance lever.
5. **Provider Tools license is restricted (Section 4.2).** The perpetual license to Provider Tools is limited to use "in connection with Client's use of the Custom Work Product." If Client later wants to migrate or modify the platform independently, it may face restrictions or additional licensing.
6. **Termination for convenience requires a 60-day notice (Section 9.1).** Client cannot exit immediately for strategic reasons; must continue paying through the notice period (and for satisfactorily performed Services under Section 9.3).
7. **Forum and choice of law favor Provider (Sections 10.2–10.3).** Arbitration in Austin, Texas and Texas governing law favor the Austin-based Provider. Client is California-based and could face inconvenience and added cost.
8. **Retainage risk on Provider side.** While Section 3.3 benefits Client, it introduces payment uncertainty for Provider that could affect cash flow and project velocity — indirectly a Client risk if Provider staffing is impacted.
9. **Annual security audit by Client is capped at "no more than once per calendar year" (Section 6.3).** Limited audit rights may be insufficient following incidents.

### Asymmetric / Unfavorable Risks for the **Provider**

1. **Late payment remedies are weak.** Section 3.2 imposes 1.5%/month interest, but Provider has no express right to suspend Services for non-payment. Continued work while unpaid creates cash-flow exposure.
2. **Broad IP indemnification (Section 8.3(ii)).** Provider must indemnify against any third-party IP infringement claims, with no carve-outs, knowledge qualifiers, or Client-mitigation obligations (e.g., modify the work product, procure a license). This is unusually broad.
3. **Open-ended warranty and conformance obligations.** Warranties run for 12 months from "final acceptance," but final acceptance is not defined — potential disputes over when the clock starts.
4. **Aggressive milestone schedule with no relief mechanism.** Forces majeure capped at 90 days (Section 11.1) — beyond that, Provider remains on the hook even for extraordinary events.
5. **Client audit and data-protection burden.** Compliance with CCPA, GDPR, and SOC 2 Type II, plus 48-hour breach notification, creates ongoing operational overhead.
6. **Client-side 10% retainage** held until final acceptance (Section 3.3) — reduces Provider's working capital throughout the project.

### Mutual / General Concerns

- **No express change-control / scope-change procedure.** Amendments require a signed writing (Section 11.4), but there is no defined process for the inevitable scope changes in a multi-phase ERP build. This invites disputes over what is included in the $2.4M fixed fee.
- **No insurance requirements specified.** No required minimums for cyber liability, professional liability, or general liability coverage.
- **No data ownership / data migration clause** addressing Client's data held by Provider during the engagement or post-termination.
- **No backup, business continuity, or disaster-recovery commitments** beyond SOC 2 Type II.
- **Confidentiality survival (5 years)** may be short for trade-secret-grade information; no perpetual protection for trade secrets.
- **Arbitration clause lacks procedural detail** (no arbitrator number, no carve-out for injunctive relief, no fee-shifting provision).
- **Non-solicitation is mutual and 12 months**, but enforcement against remote/hybrid employees can be fact-intensive.

### Financial Conditions

- **Payment terms:** Net 30 from invoice receipt.
- **Late fees:** 1.5% per month on overdue amounts, or the maximum legal rate, whichever is lower (effective annual rate ~19.6%).
- **Retainage:** Client may withhold up to **10% of each milestone payment** until final acceptance of the complete deliverable set (max total retainage $240,000).
- **Currency / taxes:** Not addressed in this Agreement — taxes and currency conversion (if any) are not specified.
- **Expenses:** No provision for reimbursable expenses — implied to be included in the fixed fee.
- **No bonuses, incentives, or liquidated damages** are specified.
- **No price escalation clause** — fixed fee applies for the entire ~20-month engagement despite multi-year labor-cost exposure.
