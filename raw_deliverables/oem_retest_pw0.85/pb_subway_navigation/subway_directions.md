# Subway Directions: Bedford Ave → 161 St-Yankee Stadium

## Route 1 — Recommended (L → 4 via Union Sq)

### Step-by-Step Directions

1. **Board the L train** at Bedford Ave, heading **westbound toward 8 Ave-14 St**.
   - Stops from Bedford Ave westbound: Bedford Ave → 1 Ave → 3 Ave → **Union Sq-14 St** → 6 Ave-14 St → 8 Ave-14 St.
   - Ride **3 stops** to **14 St-Union Sq**.

2. **Transfer at 14 St-Union Sq** from the **L** to the **4 train** (uptown/Bronx-bound).
   - Follow signs inside the station for the **4/5/6 — Uptown & Bronx** platform.
   - The L and 4 share the Union Sq complex; transfer is within the same station.

3. **Board the 4 train** (express, toward Woodlawn) at Union Sq.
   - Stops from Union Sq northbound: **14 St-Union Sq** → Grand Central-42 St → 59 St → 86 St → 125 St → 149 St-Grand Concourse → **161 St-Yankee Stadium**.
   - Note: 4 stops shown as express on this simplified map; in practice the 4 runs local north of 125 St in the Bronx, but all listed stops are served.

4. **Arrive at 161 St-Yankee Stadium** — take the exit toward River Ave / Yankee Stadium.

## Route 2 — Alternative (L → A → 4 via 14 St / Fulton St)

If the 4 is delayed or you prefer a different transfer:

1. **L westbound** from Bedford Ave to **8 Ave-14 St** (4 stops).
2. **Transfer to the A** (uptown, toward Inwood-207 St) at 14 St-8 Ave.
3. **Ride the A** northbound to **Fulton St** (stops include 34 St-Penn Station, 42 St-Port Authority, 59 St-Columbus Circle, 125 St, 168 St area — verify exact routing; A does not stop at 161 St).
4. At **Fulton St**, transfer to the **4 train** (uptown) — Fulton St is a listed transfer hub for the 4.
5. **4 northbound** from Fulton St to **161 St-Yankee Stadium** (~3–4 stops depending on routing).

**Trade-off:** More stops and an extra transfer; only use if the L→4 at Union Sq is disrupted. Prefer Route 1.

## Tips

### Best Car Position for Transfers

- **On the L (Bedford Ave → Union Sq):** Ride the **rear of the train** — the L's 14 St-Union Sq station entrance for the 4/5/6 is at the east end of the platform, closest to the back of an eastbound train from Brooklyn (and correspondingly closer to the 4 transfer from a westbound train's rear cars).
- **At Union Sq, switching to the 4:** After exiting the L, follow signs for **"4/5/6 Uptown"**. The transfer is a level walk within the same complex — no re-entry through the turnstiles if you have OMNY/MetroCard.
- **On the 4 (toward 161 St):** No critical car-position issue; any car works.

### Rush Hour Considerations

- **AM rush (7–9:30 AM):** L train into Manhattan is **extremely crowded** at Bedford Ave — let one or two trains pass if you can't squeeze on. The 4 uptown at Union Sq during AM rush is also packed but typically boarding-room.
- **PM rush (4:30–7 PM):** Reverse pattern — the **4 downtown** is brutal, but you're going **uptown**, so it's manageable. The **L westbound toward 8 Ave** at Bedford Ave in the PM is mostly outbound Brooklyn commuters and is usually easier.
- **Game days at Yankee Stadium:** Arrive **at least 45–60 minutes before first pitch**. The 161 St-Yankee Stadium station becomes extremely congested; the 4 and B/D also serve the area. Returning after the game, expect 20–30 minute waits to board a southbound 4.
- **Weekend service changes:** Check the MTA site before traveling — the L frequently runs on a modified schedule with shuttle buses between Brooklyn and Manhattan sections.
